Five readings of "three acts"

One instruction, five separate demos. Each variant below is a full page built on the version 3 simulation and its visual system, structured as three acts on the three answers given to the questions about version 1. Every variant runs here in its own frame and also opens on its own. A frame animates while any part of it is on screen and pauses when it is scrolled out; add #all to the address to keep all five running for a stress test.
